5. Deep Explanation (VERY IMPORTANT)

🔹 colspan kya karta hai?

colspan ek cell ko multiple columns ka space deta hai (horizontal merge). 

Normal:
| A | B | C |

colspan="2":
|   A   | C |

👉 A ne 2 columns le liye, isliye ek column kam ho gaya.

🔹 rowspan kya karta hai?

rowspan ek cell ko multiple rows ka space deta hai (vertical merge). 

Normal:
| A | B |
| C | D |

rowspan="2":
| A | B |
|   | D |

👉 A neeche wali row ka bhi space le raha hai.

6. Effect on Rows & Columns

  • colspan → us row mein columns kam ho jate hain
  • rowspan → next row mein cells kam likhne padte hain
  • table ka balance maintain karna zaroori hai

7. Golden Rule

✔ Har row ka total columns same hona chahiye (after merging)
